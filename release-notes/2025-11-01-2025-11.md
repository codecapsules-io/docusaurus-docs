### New

- **The Code Capsules CLI.** Our new CLI is out. See the [CLI docs](/cli/).
- **Proxy to your workloads.** Use the CLI to connect to deployed workloads from your local machine, to debug services or access your databases.

### Fixed

- Trying to scale storage down, which isn't supported, no longer causes an error.
- Scaling databases horizontally now creates the correct replicas.
