### New

- **Health checks.** Configure [health checks](/platform/capsules/health-checks/) for your capsules, so traffic only reaches replicas that are healthy and ready to serve it.
- **Docker Capsules from an image.** Create a Docker Capsule from an existing image reference. See [Deploy from an image](/products/docker-capsule/image/deploy/).
- **Static Frontend Capsules.** A new free and paid static hosting option serves your built frontend without a running workload. See [Free and Paid Plans](/products/frontend-capsule/static/plans/).

- Security enhancements.
